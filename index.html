The Incantation Is Passed Down

The Golem Goes Digital
Marvin Minsky — descendant of the Golem creator — founds the MIT Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. He builds the first neural network learning machine (SNARC), invents the head-mounted display, and becomes the "father of AI." He advises Kubrick on HAL 9000 — the cinematic Golem.
